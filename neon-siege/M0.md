## Exit criterion

The battlefield now communicates and enforces the intended defensive flow:

`Enemy spawn -> defenses -> player fallback/interception -> DATA CORE`

The V2.2 and V2.3 passes established the key gameplay rules behind that flow:

- defenses primarily intercept enemies before they cross the defensive node;
- leaked enemies remain recoverable through reduced-efficiency emergency targeting;
- building prefers the player-facing frontline instead of arbitrary empty nodes;
- the player can reposition and fire in either direction;
- enemies contacting the player cause damage and knockback, preventing cost-free body blocking;
- the DATA CORE remains the final failure condition.
