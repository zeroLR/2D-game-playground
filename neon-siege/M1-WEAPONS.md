# M1 Weapon Roster Validation

This slice implements the four initial M1 weapons from `ROADMAP.md`.

| Weapon | Intended rhythm | Tactical use |
|---|---|---|
| Pistol | Medium cadence, reliable damage | Baseline general-purpose weapon |
| SMG | Very high cadence, low per-shot damage | Sustained suppression and cleanup |
| Shotgun | Slow cadence, six-pellet spread, high knockback | Emergency breach control at close range |
| Railgun | Very slow cadence, high damage, multi-target pierce | Heavy targets and aligned enemy groups |

## Controls

Desktop weapon selection: `Z / X / C / V`.

Mobile uses the weapon selector above the tower selector.

## Playtest acceptance

- Each weapon must be recognizable by rhythm without reading its name.
- Shotgun should create visible space when the defensive line is breached.
- Railgun should reward lining up multiple enemies.
- SMG should feel substantially faster but less impactful per bullet than Pistol.
- Pistol remains the neutral baseline for future balance comparisons.
