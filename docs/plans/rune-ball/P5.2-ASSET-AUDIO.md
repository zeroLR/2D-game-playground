# Rune Ball — P5.2 Asset Audio Delivery Gate

## Why this slice exists

P5 and P5.1 established the visual hierarchy and attempted procedural Web Audio characterization, but repeated real-phone testing produced **no audible output**. The player also cannot currently inspect browser diagnostics on the test device.

P5.2 therefore changes the delivery mechanism rather than continuing to tune an inaudible synthesis path.

## Risk question

Can Rune Ball reliably deliver audible BGM and event SFX on the real phone using vendored media assets, while preserving the already-validated spectacle-forward gameplay loop?

## Delivery contract

- primary audio implementation uses `HTMLAudioElement`, not procedural Web Audio oscillators
- BGM starts from the first arena pointer interaction
- Ogg/Vorbis is preferred when the browser reports support
- MP3 siblings are compatibility fallbacks, especially for older Safari/iOS versions
- short SFX use small reusable voice pools and are primed on the first user interaction
- gameplay never waits on audio completion
- audio failure remains non-fatal

## Characterization mapping

```text
Contact          brick / mining impact
Armored contact  metallic impact
Break            sci-fi crunch explosion
Rebound          light plate impact
Vortex           low pulse + Rune confirmation
Split            high laser
Chain            laser + Rune confirmation layer
Overdrive entry  break + Rune + low pulse stack
Overdrive exit   low release accent
Results           temporary jingle hook for P6
```

BGM uses **Claimed by the Void** by vitalezzz (CC0), chosen for its cyberpunk / electronic / industrial / ambient character and its fit with Rune Ball's neon-occult arena.

Full provenance is recorded in `rune-ball/public/audio/ASSET-LICENSES.md`.

## Product learning preserved from P5

Do not use this slice to push Rune Ball back toward precision target selection.

Phone testing showed:

- once a Rune is recognized, handwriting fidelity itself is low-value polish
- causal activation feedback matters more than a perfectly readable drawn stroke
- during dense play the player is comfortable freely casting Runes and watching chain reactions resolve

P6 should test whether that spectacle-forward loop still provides enough authorship and replay motivation. P5.2 only makes the audiovisual payoff actually available.

## Build / deployment gate

`npm run build` must fail if required audio is missing:

- production Pages base remains `/2D-game-playground/rune-ball/`
- every required OGG exists under `dist/audio/` and starts with the `OggS` container signature
- every required MP3 fallback exists and is non-empty

## Phone gate

On the same device that failed P5/P5.1:

1. The first arena interaction starts audible BGM.
2. Contact and Break are clearly audible and distinct.
3. Vortex, Split, and Chain produce recognizable sound accents.
4. Overdrive entry is unmistakable by sound as well as visuals.
5. BGM does not drown collision / Rune SFX.
6. Audio remains working through a complete Overdrive cycle.

P5 cannot close until real-device audible output is confirmed.

## Scope guard

No new gameplay rule, target-selection mechanic, Rune, progression system, session timer, results UI, or final audio mastering is introduced here.
