# Rune Ball — P2.1 Rebound Utility & Chase Flow

## Why this gate exists

P2 playtesting validated the destruction loop, impact differentiation, target availability, light Combo motivation, and short multi-break satisfaction. One issue remained: wall rebound created travel time without adding a useful decision or payoff, so active mid-air swipe chasing was always preferable.

P2.1 treats that as a chase-flow problem rather than weakening the already-validated swipe controls.

## Product decision

**Swipe remains the primary control. Rebound becomes an optional re-entry accelerator.**

Do not make wall bounce mandatory and do not add swipe cooldowns, steering restrictions, or pinball-style waiting. A player must always be able to overwrite the rebound trajectory with the next swipe.

## Mechanics

### Temporary rebound boost

- wall collision applies a small authored speed bonus
- target tuning: roughly +12% at rebound
- bonus decays over roughly 0.72 seconds
- normal authored speed cap remains 440
- temporary rebound ceiling is 500
- bonus is additive and decays back out instead of permanently raising base movement speed

### Mild rebound assist

After physical reflection:

1. search existing targets inside a broad forward cone;
2. select a useful target using alignment + proximity;
3. rotate the reflected trajectory toward it by at most 18 degrees;
4. preserve speed;
5. allow the next swipe to override immediately.

This is trajectory cleanup, not auto-aim.

### Chase-aware respawn

Delayed replacement targets still use the authored spawn-anchor set. The director looks ahead across a small subset of anchors and prefers a position that:

- lies usefully along the current chase direction;
- is not too close to the ball;
- is not crowded by existing targets;
- remains at a useful travel distance.

No new random spawn architecture is introduced.

## Presentation

Do not add a permanent Rebound meter or badge.

Use existing gameplay feedback only:

- stronger directional wall flash
- short trail / glow intensity increase while rebound bonus is active
- existing development telemetry may temporarily label `REBOUND`

Score and Combo remain the only persistent gameplay HUD information in this slice.

## Playtest gate

On an actual phone, verify:

- wall contact feels like rapid re-entry into the next attack rather than waiting for the ball to return;
- swipe steering still feels fully authoritative during and immediately after rebound;
- trajectory assist is useful but not visibly auto-aimed;
- rebound does not make maximum-speed readability worse;
- useful targets remain available after breaks without obvious spawn cheating;
- P2 Crystal / Armored / Combo behavior remains intact.

If these hold, close P2 and proceed to P3 Rune Gesture Gate.
